## 1. Executive Summary of Round 2 Findings

1. **Complementarity Exploited (`Approach A` — Stacked TF-IDF + Frozen MiniLM):**
   - Combining lexical n-gram features (`TF-IDF (1,3)-grams`) with 384-dimensional dense multilingual embeddings (`paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2`) in a stacked L2-regularized classifier achieves the **highest Macro F1 on `test.jsonl`** (**`0.4422`** `[0.4028, 0.4773]`, paired $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.0579$ `[+0.0272, +0.0909]` vs. TF-IDF baseline — 95% CI excludes zero), more than doubles `gold` Macro F1 (**`0.1935`** vs. `0.0897`), and achieves a statistically significant gain on `telegram_test.jsonl` (**`0.1910`** `[0.1487, 0.2228]`, paired $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.0464$ `[+0.0105, +0.0802]`).
   - On the independent 160-item hard-negative benchmark (`hard_negatives_v2.jsonl`), Stacked TF-IDF + MiniLM achieves **`6.25%` FPR** (`10 / 160`, 95% Clopper-Pearson CI `[3.04%, 11.19%]`), down from `44.38%` (`71 / 160`) for standalone Frozen MiniLM.
2. **Retrieval over Debunk Corpus (`Approach B` — MiniLM $k$-NN + Class Centroids):**
   - Non-parametric retrieval over the 4,526 training claims using MiniLM cosine similarity ($k=15$, temperature $T=0.07$) plus class centroids cuts Frozen MiniLM's false positive rate on `hard_negatives_v2.jsonl` from `44.38%` to **`5.63%`** (`9 / 160`) while retaining strong cross-lingual generalization on `gold_unique_30` (**`0.4031` Macro F1**, paired $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.3133$ `[+0.1065, +0.4199]`) and returning human-auditable nearest training debunks.
3. **Two-Step Hierarchical Gate (`Approach C` — Binary Disinfo Gate $\rightarrow$ 12-Class Stacked Specialist):**
   - Achieves the **lowest false positive rate on `hard_negatives_v2.jsonl`** of any standalone model (**`2.50%`**, `4 / 160`, 95% Clopper-Pearson CI `[0.69%, 6.28%]`) and significantly beats TF-IDF on `test.jsonl` (**`0.4293`**, paired $\Delta\text{F1} = +0.0449$ `[+0.0127, +0.0782]`), at the cost of lower recall on out-of-domain `gold` (`0.0324`).
4. **Hybrid `NarrativeClassifierService` False-Alarm Fix:**
   - Auditing the 19 false alarms on `hard_negatives.jsonl` revealed three defects in [`backend/app/services/narrative_classifier_service.py`](file:///c:/Users/maria/scamguard/backend/app/services/narrative_classifier_service.py): substring token matching (`tok.startswith(kw) or kw in tok` matching `"сво"` inside `"свою"`), double-counting lexical stem hits as both `distinct_kw_hits` and coarse lexicon `proto_sim >= 0.35`, and uncorroborated rule overrides when the ML model predicted low class probability (`ml_prob < 0.30`).
   - Fixing token matching and requiring ML corroboration (`ml_prob >= 0.30`) on multi-clause posts (`len(tokens) >= 12`) reduced Hybrid FPR on `hard_negatives.jsonl` from **`15.83%` (`19 / 120`) to `0.00%` (`0 / 120`, CP CI `[0.00%, 3.03%]`)** and on `hard_negatives_v2.jsonl` from **`29.38%` (`47 / 160`) to `3.12%` (`5 / 160`, CP CI `[1.02%, 7.14%]`)**, while **increasing `test.jsonl` Macro F1 from `0.1753` to `0.2638`**.
5. **Step 2.5 `SimilarityService` Upgrade to Dense Multilingual Embeddings:**
   - Switching `SimilarityService` default from the 15-concept-group `DeterministicMultilingualEmbedder` to `SentenceTransformerEmbedder` (`paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2`, behind config flag `SCAMGUARD_USE_DENSE_SIMILARITY_EMBEDDER=true`) with calibrated cross-lingual thresholds (`RO-RU = 0.50`) and token-level Russian negation / numeric Detail Guard rules improves 317-pair F1 from **`0.7152` to `0.9700`** (`RO-RU` cross-lingual F1 from **`0.6667` to `0.8667`**).
6. **Production Classifier Status (Per Pre-Registered Decision Rule in [`docs/decisions.md`](file:///c:/Users/maria/scamguard/docs/decisions.md)):**
   - Because `telegram_test.jsonl` labels (`N=150`) were generated by a keyword script (`label_telegram_messages.py`), [`docs/decisions.md`](file:///c:/Users/maria/scamguard/docs/decisions.md) requires evaluating on human-annotated Telegram messages ([`ml/data/labeling/telegram_blind_batch.csv`](file:///c:/Users/maria/scamguard/ml/data/labeling/telegram_blind_batch.csv)) before swapping the default `.joblib` artifact. Thus `narrative_classifier.joblib` (with the fixed Hybrid gate, `3.12%` FPR on `v2`) remains the active default while `stacked_tfidf_minilm.joblib` and `twostep_tfidf_minilm.joblib` are trained, saved, and ready for one-command human-label evaluation via [`ml/scripts/evaluate_human_labels.py`](file:///c:/Users/maria/scamguard/ml/scripts/evaluate_human_labels.py).

## 6. Known Limitations & Honest Methodological Constraints

1. **Weakly Labeled Telegram Test Set (`telegram_test.jsonl`):** All 400 messages in `telegram_labeled_400.jsonl` (`250` train, `150` test) were labeled by a deterministic keyword heuristic script (`ml/scripts/label_telegram_messages.py`). Until the 150-message blind human annotation batch (`ml/data/labeling/telegram_blind_batch.csv`) is completed and evaluated via `ml/scripts/evaluate_human_labels.py`, scores on `telegram_test.jsonl` remain weak estimates.
2. **Effective Sample Size of `gold.jsonl` ($N = 30$ Unique Claims):** As documented in `docs/ml-round2-audit.md`, `gold.jsonl` (`N = 150`) was constructed in `ml/scripts/build_dataset.py:337-413` by repeating 30 hardcoded single-sentence claims 5 times (`gold_samples * 5`). Therefore, the true independent sample size is $N = 30$ (`gold_unique_30`), which yields wide 95% confidence intervals (`±0.15–0.20` Macro F1).
3. **Contamination in Legacy `hard_negatives.jsonl` (`v1`):** All 120 items in `ml/eval/hard_negatives.jsonl` also appear in `train.jsonl`, and items `71–120` are `FAKEROM` records misassigned to Category `10` due to a `label: null` fallback bug (`build_dataset.py:265`). Always use `ml/eval/hard_negatives_v2.jsonl` ($N = 160$, 0% training overlap) with exact Clopper-Pearson confidence intervals for false-positive evaluation.
4. **CPU Fine-Tuning vs. Full GPU Fine-Tuning:** Round 1 fine-tuning of `paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` was restricted to 1 seed, 2 epochs, and 4 unfrozen layers on CPU, which degraded cross-lingual representation quality on `gold` (`0.1282` vs. `0.4679` frozen). Full 3-seed GPU fine-tuning of `xlm-roberta-base` and `multilingual-e5-base` is provided in `ml/notebooks/finetune_transformers_gpu.ipynb`.
5. **Channel Diversity in Local Telegram Corpus:** The local SQLite database (`backend/scamguard.db`) contains 934 messages from two public channels (`@realmorarinews` and `@wtfmoldova`). Expanding coverage across 20+ diverse Moldovan channels is needed to evaluate rare narrative classes (`Categories 5, 8, 9, 11, 12`).
